CHILD LABOUR ACADEMY

About the Program

Students in the Child Labour Academy program will perform unpaid labour for the School, it's administration, and its partnering orginizations.



Course Descriptions

HIST201 Introduction to Child Labour +

Students will be introducted to the fundamental concepts of Child Labour, its history, and the lengthy legal battle to ensure its legalization.

CHIL101 Unpaided Internship +

Expanding on concepts of the previous semester, Students will be tasked with cleaning dishes, preparing food, repairing machinary, and other miscellaneous jobs around the school — without pay —.

CHIL102 Introduction to Coal Mining +

Advancing further from their Junior year, Students will leave the classroom and explore the local coal mine. Students will perform hard work and gain valuable experience working in a Coal mine — without pay —. Upon completion of the semester long internship students will then compile all that they have learned into a final project portfolio to present infront of the entire school.

CHIL103 Child Labour Management +

In their final semester, Seniors will be tasked with managing and overseeing the Juniors in the CHIL101. Seniors will ensure maximum productivity through grade deductions, write-ups, and other disciplinary measures.
